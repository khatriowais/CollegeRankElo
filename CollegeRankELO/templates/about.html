About the Project

Why Elo?

Traditional rankings are static — a college can sit at #12 for years even as its placements collapse. Elo is dynamic: every pairwise comparison updates ratings in real time.

Formulas

Expected(A) = 1 / (1 + 10^((R_B - R_A) / 400))
R_A_new    = R_A + K * (S_A - Expected(A))
