📖 Methodology

How the ranking works

Two ideas combined: a weighted score decides who wins a head-to-head from real data, then Elo integrates those wins into a living leaderboard.

♟️ The Elo update

Same maths as chess rankings (FIDE K = {{ k }}). Beating a stronger college earns more points than beating a weaker one.

Expected(A) = 1 / (1 + 10^((R_B − R_A) / 400))
R_A_new     = R_A + K × (S_A − Expected(A))

A 400-point gap ≈ 91% win probability. Underdogs gain more for an upset.

1 = win, 0.5 = draw, 0 = loss. Zero-sum: what A gains, B loses.

Both new ratings + full score breakdown are stored for audit and replay.

Viva cheat-sheet

Why Elo over averages?
Averages are static. Elo is pairwise and dynamic — a college climbs by consistently beating peers just above it.

Why K = {{ k }}?
FIDE standard for developing players: reactive enough to reflect new placement data, stable enough not to swing wildly.

What stops the rich getting richer?
Winners are decided from real attributes, Elo only integrates results. Recalculate replays history deterministically after tuning.

Production-ready?
Secrets live in .env, SQLAlchemy runs on SQLite or Postgres via DATABASE_URL, and every rating change is auditable.
